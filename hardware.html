Hardware Integration

Complete wiring guide for the Raspberry Pi, GPS Module, and peripherals.

SAFETY PRECAUTIONS

• The NEO-6M GPS Module requires 5V power. Do not connect it to 3.3V.
• The OLED Display requires 3.3V power. Do not connect it to 5V.
• Always use a 220Ω Resistor with the LED.
• Double-check TX and RX connections (Cross-wiring) before powering on.
